My research focuses on how individuals, communities, and institutions respond to environmental risks in the context of climate change. I study how risk communication, policy design, and governance frameworks influence adaptation decisions, particularly in areas such as flood risk management, nature-based solutions, and agricultural responses to climate variability. My work combines empirical data, experiments, and policy analysis to understand how environmental risks are perceived and managed in practice. Ultimately, I aim to contribute to the design of policies that support effective and socially equitable climate adaptation.
